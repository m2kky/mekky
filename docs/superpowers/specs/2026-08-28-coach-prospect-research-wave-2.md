## 1. Ahmed Adel

### What is already working

- Career and employability coach associated with KUN Academy.
- His public LinkedIn presence is unusually large for this list: roughly 152K followers, with a substantial content archive.
- Publicly offers career coaching, CV and LinkedIn work, cover letters, interview preparation, and job-search support.
- His posts use direct-message calls to action and refer to delivery by "me and my team," which suggests an existing service operation rather than a solo hobby practice.

### Commercial diagnosis

Ahmed does not need help getting attention. His likely bottleneck is turning a large social audience and manual DMs into a controlled service journey. A useful product would let a visitor choose the right service, complete a diagnostic intake, pay, book, and be routed to the right team member.

### Outreach angle

Do not say: "I noticed you don't have a website."  
Say: his LinkedIn is already doing the hard acquisition work, and there is an opportunity to make the next step feel as professional and scalable as the audience he has built.

### Strong observation for the email

"Your audience can currently ask for CV, interview, LinkedIn, or career-coaching help through messages. I mapped a shorter journey that could qualify the request, recommend the right service, and move the person directly into payment and booking."

### Best proof

Ahmed Rammah: intake, booking, international payment, and an operational platform rather than a brochure site.

## Message strategy resulting from the research

Each email should contain only four substantive moves:

1. A precise observation that proves we studied the recipient.
2. One concrete client journey or commercial idea—not a list of features.
3. One relevant case-study image and a single sentence explaining why it is relevant.
4. A low-pressure link to the discovery call, framed as optional.

The calendar link can stay, but it should be the secondary action after an invitation to reply. For a cold email, a direct calendar-only CTA asks for too much commitment. Recommended close:

> If the idea is relevant, reply and I will send the one-page journey I mapped. If a conversation is easier, you can choose a suitable time here.
